ModelDistl derives from Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 by the Qwen Team.
The original model is distributed under the Apache License 2.0.
The release includes that license as Qwen-LICENSE.txt.
https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/tree/cdbee75f17c01a7cc42f958dc650907174af0554

ModelDistl trained a rank-16 linear adapter on verified responses from
OpenAI GPT-6.2 Astra. It fuses the adapter with the original BF16
weights and quantizes the result for local llama.cpp inference.
Source data, attribution and the evaluation protocol are in the repository.
https://github.com/shiv-eshwar/modeldistl/blob/astra-v0.2.0/runs/astra-v2/PROTOCOL.md
